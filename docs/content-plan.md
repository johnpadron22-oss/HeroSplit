## Comment Reply Templates

### "Is this free?"
> "Yes! 4 complete programs are free — no credit card, no trial. Night Acrobat, Agile Climber, Solar Sentinel, and Night Detective are all free forever. Pro unlocks 36 more programs for $4.99/mo or $39.99/yr."

### "What equipment do I need?"
> "Night Acrobat is 100% bodyweight — no equipment at all. Agile Climber needs dumbbells. Solar Sentinel is gym-based (barbells/cables). There's a quiz in the app that recommends the right one for your setup."

### "How many days per week?"
> "Depends on the program — Night Acrobat and Night Detective are 3–4 days, Agile Climber is 4 days, Solar Sentinel is 4–5 days. The quiz recommends based on your schedule."

### "Is this good for beginners?"
> "Night Acrobat and Night Detective are beginner-friendly — no gym needed, the app walks you through every exercise. The quiz will point you to the right one based on your experience."

### "What is a 'split'?"
> "A split is just how you divide your workouts across the week — like upper body one day, lower body the next (upper/lower split), or push exercises vs pull exercises (push/pull). HeroSplit labels each program clearly so you always know what to expect."

### "How do I cancel?"
> "Cancel anytime from your profile → Pro Subscription → Manage. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. No fees, no tricks."

### "Is this on iOS/Android?"
> "It's a web app that works on any phone browser — just open hero-split.vercel.app and add it to your home screen for the app feel. Android TWA is in progress. iOS App Store submission coming soon."

### "Is my data private?"
> "Your workout data stays on our servers (Supabase, based in the US). We don't sell your data, don't track you with ads, and you can delete your account at any time from the app settings. Full policy at hero-split.vercel.app/privacy"

### "How much does Pro cost?"
> "Monthly: $4.99/month. Annual: $39.99/year (save 33%). You can try the free programs first — no card needed. Upgrade from inside the app anytime."

### "What's the difference between Hero and Villain programs?"
> "Hero programs are structured, evidence-based training plans (strength, hypertrophy, athletic performance). Villain programs are higher intensity, higher volume, and built for people who want to push limits. All villain programs are Pro only."
