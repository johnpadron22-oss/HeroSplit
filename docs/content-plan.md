## Growth Notes

- **"What app is this?" is the most valuable comment.** Respond within the first hour to every one — it signals to the algorithm that your video drives conversations.
- **Use app screen recordings, not just gym footage.** Seeing the XP animation and rank-up is the strongest hook for conversions.
- **Streak content performs best on Monday** (people starting the week fresh, accountability mindset).
- **Villain unlock content performs best on Sunday** (treat yourself mentality, people browsing before a new week).
- **Never name original characters in captions.** If you want to evoke a vibe, describe the aesthetic: "dark vigilante energy", "titan-level strength", "cursed power output".
